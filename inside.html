Inside the app

Everything the league needs, in one place.

⏱

Draft room

A live board with the pick clock, who's on the clock, recent picks, and undo for when somebody fat-fingers the wrong guard.

▦

Player pool

The full field with points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks. Filter by position or seed, switch between list and card view, sort by best available.

◈

Teams & rosters

See every manager's roster at a glance, so you know exactly who took the sleeper you were sitting on.

▲

Standings

Ranked totals with a category breakdown, plus points remaining — the maximum you can still score if every team you own wins out.

⌇

Bracket view

The full bracket for whichever tournament you picked, with your drafted players marked so you can watch your season end in real time.

✉

League chat

Trash talk stays in the app instead of getting buried in a group text about somebody's weekend plans.

◫

Latest updates

A college basketball news feed inside the app, filtered to the teams in your tournament so you catch an injury before you draft the guy.

◷

Scheduled drafts

Set a date and time and every manager sees the same countdown on their home screen. No group text trying to pin down a Tuesday.

⚙

Custom scoring

Turn categories on or off and set your own weights. Run it straight, or make blocks worth triple and watch the draft strategy change.

◔

Pick timer & alerts

Set the clock to whatever your league can tolerate. Turn on browser notifications and get pinged when it's your turn, even with the app closed.

⌂

Installs like an app

Add it to your home screen from the browser. Full screen, its own icon, no app store and no update prompts.

⇄

Autopick & skip

Somebody late to the draft? Autopick takes the best available on their behalf, or skip them and come back around.
