Why it exists

Your bracket dies on day one.

Every March, the big platforms hand you the same thing: a bracket. You fill it out, a 12-seed wins on Thursday afternoon, and you have nothing left to watch for two weeks. There was never a real fantasy draft for the tournament. So we built one.

The old way

Bracket pool

One set of predictions, locked before the first tip.

  • Everyone picks the same favorites
  • Two upsets and you're mathematically out
  • No decisions to make after Thursday
  • You root for chalk, not for players
Tipoff Fantasy

Snake draft league

A real draft, a real roster, and a reason to watch every game.

  • Every player is owned by exactly one manager
  • Nobody has the same team as you
  • A deep run by your 11-seed sleeper wins it
  • You're live in every game your roster survives

Get your league drafting.

Create a league, send one link, and have a full snake draft running tonight.
