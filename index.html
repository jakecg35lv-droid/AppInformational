How it works

Three steps. One night.

01

Create a league

Pick a tournament, set your scoring weights and pick timer, then send one link. Your friends are in with a six-character code.

02

Snake draft

Take turns picking individual players. Pick last in round one and you pick first in round two. Every roster needs a guard, a forward and a center.

03

Watch it score

Real box scores update your standings while the games are on. Your players keep earning until their team goes home.

Live scoring

Your roster is
always on.

Points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks convert to fantasy points using weights your commissioner controls. Scores move while the game is being played, not the next morning.

FPTS = (PTS × 1.0) + (REB × 1.2) + (AST × 1.5) + (STL × 2.0) + (BLK × 2.0)
