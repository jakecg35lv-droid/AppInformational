Why it exists

Your bracket dies on day one.

Every March, the big platforms hand you the same thing: a bracket. You fill it out, a 12-seed wins on Thursday afternoon, and you have nothing left to watch for two weeks. There was never a real fantasy draft for the tournament. So we built one.

The old way

Bracket pool

One set of predictions, locked before the first tip.

  • Everyone picks the same favorites
  • Two upsets and you're mathematically out
  • No decisions to make after Thursday
  • You root for chalk, not for players
Tipoff Fantasy

Snake draft league

A real draft, a real roster, and a reason to watch every game.

  • Every player is owned by exactly one manager
  • Nobody has the same team as you
  • A deep run by your 11-seed sleeper wins it
  • You're live in every game your roster survives
How it works

Five steps, start to trophy.

The whole thing takes about twenty minutes to set up and one evening to draft.

01

Create the league

Commissioner · 2 min

Name it, set how many managers can join, and pick how many rounds you want to draft. The app generates a six-character league code and an invite link.

02

Everyone joins

Managers · one tap

Send the link. Your friends enter the code, and they're in — no app store, no downloads. The draft order is set and visible to everyone before you start.

03

Choose the tournament

Commissioner

Pick the event your league is playing for — an eight-team in-season tournament like Maui or Battle 4 Atlantis, or the full 64-team postseason field. The player pool and bracket load to match.

04

Snake draft

Everyone · ~45 min

Schedule it in advance and everyone gets a countdown, or start it on the spot. Take turns on a timer, filter the pool, sort by best available, and lock your pick. Order reverses each round so the last pick in round one gets the first pick in round two.

05

Watch the standings move

Through the final

Your roster scores in every game it plays. As teams get knocked out, your points remaining shrinks — and the manager who drafted the deepest run takes it.

Scoring

Five categories. Your weights.

Every stat line your players produce converts into fantasy points using the weights your commissioner sets. These are the defaults — assists and defense are worth more than raw scoring, so the twenty-point guard who does nothing else isn't an automatic first-rounder.

Any category can be switched off entirely. A league that only counts rebounds and blocks is a completely different draft.

Questions

Before you commission.

What's a snake draft?

Managers pick in order, then the order reverses each round. If you draft fourth out of four, you pick 4th and 5th back-to-back. It keeps an early pick from being a permanent advantage, which matters here because the talent drop-off after the top seeds is steep.

What happens when my player's team loses?

They stop scoring. Whatever they banked stays on your total, but they produce nothing further. This is the whole game: the standings page shows your points remaining, so you can see exactly how much upside you have left compared to everyone else.

Which tournaments can we play?

Eight-team in-season events like the Maui Invitational and Battle 4 Atlantis, plus the full 64-team postseason bracket. The commissioner picks one when the league is set up, and the player pool and bracket load to match that field.

How many people do I need?

It works with as few as two and comfortably handles a normal group of eight to twelve. More managers means thinner rosters and a much harder draft, which is usually the fun version.

Do we have to draft the same night?

You draft together in one sitting, but you can schedule it ahead of time — set a date and everyone gets a countdown. The commissioner can pause and resume, autopick covers anyone who can't make it, and there's a skip control for a manager who's stalling.

Does it cost anything?

No. There's no entry fee, no subscription, and no money handling of any kind inside the app. If your league plays for something, that's between you.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen from the share menu, and it opens full screen with its own icon — same feel as a native app, no app store involved.

Can I redo the draft?

The commissioner can restart the league, which clears every pick and resets to round one, pick one. Managers, settings, and the league code all stay the same.
