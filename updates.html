September 2026 Latest

Roster rules and a draft that protects itself

Drafting eight of the highest-scoring players available was never much of a decision. Now every roster has to hold a real shape, and the draft room looks out for you when you are not looking at it.

  • Position requirements. Every roster needs at least one guard, one forward and one center. Five of your eight picks stay completely free, and a player only locks when taking him would make a legal roster impossible.
  • Smarter autopick. If your timer runs out, the pick made for you can no longer leave you with an illegal roster.
  • Short-handed draft warning. Starting a draft before the league fills now tells the commissioner exactly how many seats are still empty.
  • Commissioner-only league rules. Scoring weights and the pick timer can only be changed by the commissioner, and every change is written to the league activity feed.
September 2026

Your account, your leagues, your alerts

  • Account controls. Change your email, change your password, or delete your account outright from Settings.
  • My Leagues. Every league you have joined lives in one list. Switch between them in a tap.
  • Notifications you choose. Six separate alerts instead of one switch: draft starting soon, a pick made, your player scoring, your team eliminated, a standings change, a new manager joining.
  • Your color. Pick an accent that marks your picks and your standings row, so you can find yourself at a glance.
  • A privacy policy worth reading. Plain language on what we collect, what we never collect, and how to delete it.
September 2026

Live stat pipeline

The machinery that turns real box scores into fantasy points. It polls only while tournaments are actually being played, so it is not burning through data in July.

Summer 2026

Tournaments, brackets and scoring you control

  • Pick your tournament. Maui Invitational, Battle 4 Atlantis and more, each with its own field and bracket.
  • Live bracket. See who is still alive, and which of your players are still earning.
  • Custom scoring. Five categories, weights your commissioner sets, any of them switchable off. A league that only counts rebounds and blocks is a completely different draft.
  • Latest Updates feed. College basketball news inside the app.
  • Install it like an app. Add Tipoff to your home screen and it opens full screen, works offline, and can alert you when you are on the clock.
