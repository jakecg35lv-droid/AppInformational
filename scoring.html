Scoring

Five categories. Your weights.

Every stat line your players produce converts into fantasy points using the weights your commissioner sets. These are the defaults — assists and defense are worth more than raw scoring, so the twenty-point guard who does nothing else isn't an automatic first-rounder.

Any category can be switched off entirely. A league that only counts rebounds and blocks is a completely different draft.

FPTS = (PTS × 1.0) + (REB × 1.2) + (AST × 1.5) + (STL × 2.0) + (BLK × 2.0)
