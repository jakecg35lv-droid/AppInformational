How it plays

Seven shots. Make them count.

Every prompt has a lot of right answers. The question is how deep you're willing to go before the shot clock runs out.

01

Read the prompt

Each one asks for a player, team or coach that fits. Plenty of answers work. Some are worth a lot more than others.

02

Beat the shot clock

You get 25 seconds per prompt. Type a name and fire before the buzzer, or the possession is gone.

03

Go deep for points

The answer everyone says is a layup for 10. The name only real fans remember is a deep cut for 85.

04

Find the Half Court

Every prompt hides one answer worth 100. Hit it on all seven and you post a perfect 700.

Scoring

The deeper the cut, the deeper the shot.

Every correct answer lands in a zone based on how few people would think of it. Popular answers still score, so you're never shut out. You just can't win the day on layups.
